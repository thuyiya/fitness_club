# 00 --- Product Vision

## Target customer

Small gyms, personal trainers, independent fitness coaches and coaches
working across multiple gym locations.

The product should work especially well when a coach has roughly 10--200
active members and does not have dedicated operations staff.

## Problem

Small gym coaches often use a mixture of:

-   WhatsApp
-   spreadsheets
-   notes
-   paper plans
-   generic workout apps
-   food photos
-   Apple Health
-   manual weigh-ins

This creates fragmented information and makes it difficult to understand
who is progressing, who needs attention and what each member should do
today.

## Solution

Create a simple coach/member system where:

-   coaches create and manage gyms
-   coaches add members
-   members belong to one or more coach-managed gyms according to the
    business rules
-   coaches create workout and meal plans
-   coaches save reusable templates
-   coaches assign plans to members
-   members complete/log activities
-   health/activity data can sync from Apple Health
-   members can send meal photos and other progress information
-   coach and member communicate in chat
-   the coach receives a concise daily summary and attention list

## Success metrics

### Coach

-   Time required to create a member plan
-   Number of active members
-   Plan completion rate
-   Member check-in rate
-   Members needing attention
-   Reuse rate of templates

### Member

-   Daily active use
-   Workout completion
-   Meal logging consistency
-   Weekly check-ins
-   Plan adherence
-   Weight/progress tracking

## Non-goals for MVP

-   Full gym POS
-   Payroll
-   Advanced accounting
-   Class booking marketplace
-   Public social network
-   Complex supplement marketplace
-   AI-generated medical/nutrition diagnosis
-   Enterprise multi-company billing
