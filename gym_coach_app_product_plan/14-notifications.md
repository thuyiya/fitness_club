# 14 --- Notifications

## Member notifications

-   New workout plan assigned
-   New meal plan assigned
-   Workout reminder
-   Meal/check-in reminder
-   Coach message
-   Plan changed

## Coach notifications

-   Member message
-   Member needs attention
-   Missed workout threshold
-   Plan ending
-   Member completed workout
-   Member submitted meal/photo

## Notification controls

Members and coaches should be able to configure notification categories.

Avoid excessive notifications.

## Deep links

Every notification should open the relevant screen.

Example:

`Coach message → Chat conversation`

`Workout reminder → Today's workout`
