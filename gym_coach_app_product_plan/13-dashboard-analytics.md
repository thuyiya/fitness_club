# 13 --- Dashboards & Analytics

## Coach Home

The home screen should answer:

**What needs my attention today?**

Top cards:

-   Active members
-   Workouts completed today
-   Meals logged today
-   Members needing attention

Then:

-   Today's member activity
-   Recent messages
-   Quick actions

## Member Home

Answer:

**What should I do today?**

Show:

-   current plan
-   today's workout
-   today's meals
-   calories/activity
-   steps
-   progress snapshot
-   coach message

## Member progress

Charts:

-   weight
-   steps
-   active calories
-   workouts
-   nutrition consistency
-   body measurements

## Coach member summary

For each member:

`This week: 4/5 workouts · 17 meals logged · 42,300 steps`

Use simple trends and status indicators instead of overwhelming
dashboards.

## Analytics philosophy

Surface decisions, not data dumps.

Example:

`Kasun has missed 2 workouts this week`

is more useful than a screen containing 15 disconnected charts.
