## MVP launch definition

A coach can:

1.  Register
2.  Create multiple gyms
3.  Add members
4.  Create/reuse a workout plan
5.  Create/reuse a meal plan
6.  Assign both to a member
7.  See the member's daily activity
8.  Chat with the member

A member can:

1.  Register/join a coach
2.  See today's plan
3.  Complete workouts
4.  Log meals/photos
5.  Track weight/progress
6.  Connect Apple Health
7.  Chat with the coach
