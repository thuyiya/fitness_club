# 10 --- Daily Logging

## Member can log

### Activity

-   workout
-   steps
-   active calories
-   duration

### Nutrition

-   meal
-   meal photo
-   calories
-   macros if known
-   water

### Body

-   weight
-   body fat if available
-   measurements
-   progress photos

### Optional wellbeing

-   energy
-   mood
-   sleep quality

Do not make optional wellbeing fields mandatory.

## One-tap philosophy

The Home screen should have quick actions:

`Log Workout` · `Log Meal` · `Scan/Photo` · `Weight`

## Coach view

The coach should see a daily member summary:

-   workout done/not done
-   meals logged
-   active calories
-   steps
-   latest weight
-   messages
-   attention flags
