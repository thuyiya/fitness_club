# 09 --- Plan Templates

Templates are a major time-saving feature for small gym coaches.

## Template types

-   Workout only
-   Meal only
-   Combined workout + nutrition
-   Goal-specific program

## Example templates

-   Fat Loss Beginner --- 4 weeks
-   Muscle Gain Beginner --- 8 weeks
-   Strength Foundation --- 6 weeks
-   Home Workout --- 4 weeks
-   Mobility --- 3 weeks
-   General Fitness --- 4 weeks

## Template workflow

`Create Template → Save → Duplicate → Customize → Assign`

Never force a coach to edit the original template when assigning it.

Create a member-specific copy/version.

## Template metadata

-   name
-   goal
-   difficulty
-   duration
-   author
-   gym
-   tags
-   visibility
-   version
