## Information on your device

The app stores the profile and goals you enter, nutrition and activity logs, saved plans, AI conversations, and journal photos and notes in its local app storage. It uses this information to provide plans, logging, and progress views. The app does not upload these contents to a remote AI service. No app account is required.

## Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads steps, active energy, exercise time, walking/running distance, workouts, and body mass from Apple Health to display your progress. Access is read-only. HealthKit readings are held in memory while the app displays them; Apple Health remains their persistent source. We do not use HealthKit data for advertising or sell it. You can disconnect in the app and revoke access through Apple Health or device settings. Disconnecting does not delete records from Apple Health.

## Photos and camera

You choose whether to take or select a photo. Saved journal images are copied to the app's local storage. The app does not perform remote photo analysis. Deleting a journal entry removes its copy from the app; it does not remove the original from your Photos library or reverse nutrition totals already logged.

## AI downloads and app updates

AI coaching requires an initial model download of approximately 940 MB. The app may start this download on Wi-Fi and provides model controls in Settings. Once downloaded, AI responses are generated on-device.

Model downloads connect to Hugging Face and its delivery providers. App updates may connect to Expo's update service. These services receive information needed for network requests, such as IP address and request metadata. Their handling is subject to their own policies. ## Your choices and deletion

You can delete individual journal photos and remove the AI model in Settings. Reset health profile resets your profile and goals; it does not erase every saved log, conversation, plan or photo. Deleting the app through device settings removes its local app data; offloading the app is different and may retain data. Device or iCloud backups and original Photos-library items are managed separately by you and Apple. No in-app cloud-sync service is provided.
